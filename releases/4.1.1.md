# 4.1.1

Existing-project manager replies now reach the normal delivery and discussion tools instead of
being consumed by a pre-assignment initial-team staffing commitment. A retained project assignment
also retires that obsolete staffing callback through the normal personal-work result, after current
management checks. Stale management is reported and cannot trigger replacement-team design.

Validation: regression cases cover current and stale management and retain the initial-team path
for managers without a project assignment. No permission or SDK dependency changes.
